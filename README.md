# Bool Pgia Game

A small C# WinForms Bool Pgia game. It started as a C#/.NET course project.

## Features

- Choose between 4 and 10 guesses.
- Pick colors to guess a four-color sequence.
- Get feedback after each guess and see the sequence when the game ends.
- Play again after a win or loss.

## How to play

Enter the number of guesses (4–10) and click **Start**.

<img width="964" height="667" alt="Start screen" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/09e09a1f-ce48-4b39-8699-c815fb767b90" />

Click the four buttons in a row to choose colors. Their order matters. Click the arrow on the left to submit your guess.

<img width="591" height="507" alt="Choosing colors for a guess" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f1bf3574-3e16-4fa6-a56e-81b52dacb8f8" />

The feedback buttons on the left show:

- **Yellow:** correct color, wrong position.
- **Black:** correct color, correct position.

<img width="588" height="510" alt="Feedback after a guess" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f9f79de1-0e9e-4e45-891b-66ec55c155bd" />

When the game ends, the sequence is revealed. You can then choose to play again or close the game.

<img width="595" height="513" alt="Revealed sequence at game over" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f9c9b92f-dfeb-4db9-b963-fb948a1daa89" />
